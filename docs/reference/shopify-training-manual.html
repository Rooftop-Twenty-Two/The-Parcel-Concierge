Who this is for

Anyone at The Parcel Concierge who adds or edits products in Shopify. You do not need to understand the website to use it. Fill in the product, apply the tags from section 5, save. Everything after that happens by itself.

1  How a product reaches a page

Three steps. You only ever touch the first one.

The chain, start to finish
1. You tag the product. → You add All Hampers to a new hamper. This is your job, and the only part that is.
2. The collection collects it. → The hamper collection is set to gather anything tagged "All Hampers" automatically. Nobody adds it by hand.
3. The page shows it. → The website asks that collection what is in it, and builds /hampers from the answer.

So a tag is not a label on a shelf. A tag is what puts the product on the shelf. Get the tags right and the pages look after themselves.

2  What you do, and what you must not do

✓  Please do

  • Add products in the Shopify admin, one at a time
  • Edit a product's title, description, images, price and stock
  • Apply the tags in section 5
  • Fix a tag that is wrong or missing
  • Unpublish a product you no longer sell
  • Tell us when something does not appear where you expected

✗  Please do not

  • Do not create, edit or delete collections
  • Do not import products by CSV
  • Do not add a product to a collection by hand
  • Do not change a product's handle
  • Do not remove existing tags to tidy up
  • Do not use type:builder-item on anything

2.1  Why not collections

Do not add collections

The website reads a fixed set of collections by their handle — the short name in the web address, not the title on screen. Handles are unforgiving:

  • Shopify quietly adds -1 or -2 when a handle is taken. A collection at the wrong handle is invisible to the website, and looks completely normal in the admin.
  • Renaming a collection does not change its handle. This is why the hamper collection is titled "General Hampers 2026" but its handle is still christmas-2026.
  • A manual collection cannot be converted to an automated one afterwards. It has to be deleted and rebuilt — and by then the handle is taken.
  • Deleting a collection darks its page on the site immediately.

A new collection is a change to the website, not a change to Shopify. Tell us what you want and we will set it up with its page in the same release.

2.2  Why not CSV imports

Do not import products

A product CSV import is the fastest way to damage the catalogue, and the damage is quiet:

  • A CSV has no collections column. So an import can never put a product on a page, no matter how it is filled in. People assume it has worked and it has not.
  • If the "Overwrite any current products that have the same handle" box is left unticked, Shopify silently skips every product that already exists. It reports success. Almost nothing changes. This has already cost us once — a wine collection came back with 314 products instead of 373.
  • If it is ticked and a column is blank, the blank overwrites the real value. One missing column can wipe descriptions, images or prices across thousands of products.
  • An import cannot be undone.

If you need to change many products at once, send us the list. We do it against a backup, in a way that can be reversed.

What this leaves you

Everything that matters day to day: adding products, writing them properly, pricing them, photographing them and tagging them. The tags are the steering wheel — sections 5 and 6 are the whole job.

3  The six rules

1
Every product needs at least one collection tag. Without one it reaches no page. It will still turn up in site search, which is why this is easy to miss.
2
Never remove existing tags. Tags like "French Wines" and "Chenin Blanc Wine" are not clutter — they are what fills the Country and Grape filters today. Add to them; never tidy them away.
3
Spelling and capitals must match exactly. The collection tags in section 5.1 are matched literally. All Hampers works; "all hampers" and "All Hamper" do not.
4
A page needs four products to open. Under four, it redirects to its parent. Deliberate — an empty page is worse than no page.
5
Out of stock means off the site. Listings and search hide anything unavailable. You do not need to unpublish it.
6
Changes take up to an hour to appear. The site caches pages for speed. Nothing is broken — wait, or ask us to clear it.

  1. Title, description, images.
  2. Price and inventory.

5.5  The alcohol tag

TagWhat it does
contains:alcohol Arms the over-18 gate when the item goes into a Build a Gift box, and marks a corporate gift as alcoholic. Put it on beer, cider, liqueurs, liqueur chocolates, Christmas puddings with brandy, and any hamper containing alcohol. This is the tag missed most often, and the one with legal weight.

5.6  Wine description tags

Plain words, no prefix. These fill the Country, Region and Grape filters. The website is forgiving here — case, accents and a trailing "Wine" all work, so "french wine", "French Wines" and "France" all count as France.

6  Which tag sends it where

Find your row. Apply every tag in it. That is the whole job.

A finished hamper or gift box sold as one product
Tags: All Hampers   type:hamper   contains:alcohol (if it holds any)   plus occasion: and recipient: as they apply
Appears on: /hampers · its price band (automatic, from the price) · /occasions/christmas · the homepage hamper band · search
Wine, Champagne, port or sherry
Tags: Fine Wines + its style tag (Red Wines / White Wines / Rose Wine / Champagne and Prosecco / Port and Sherry / Organic Wine / Non-Alcoholic / Wine Gift Sets)
  type:wine   contains:alcohol   plus country, region and grape
Appears on: /wine · its style page · its price band (automatic) · Build a Gift wine shelf
A spirit or liqueur
Tags: Fine Wines   type:spirit   contains:alcohol
Anything else — chocolate, candles, crystal, baby, cards, homeware, food
Tags: All Gifts + one shelf tag from section 7   type:addon   contains:alcohol (if it holds any)
Appears in: Build a Gift, on "Everything" and on your shelf · search
Why there is no gifts section

The site sells hampers and wine as its products. Everything else is sold as something you put into a hamper, through Build a Gift. A chocolate bar is not missing because it has no gifts page — it is on the chocolate shelf of the builder, which is where it belongs.

9  It is not showing up

Work down in order. This is sorted by how often each one turns out to be the cause.

#CheckFix
1Does it have a collection tag from 5.1?Nine times out of ten this is it. Add the right one from section 6.
2Is the tag spelt exactly right?Capitals and spacing are matched literally. "all hampers" does nothing.
3Has an hour passed?Pages cache for up to an hour. Wait, or ask us to clear it.
4Is it in stock?Zero stock hides it from every listing and from search.
5Is it published?Check the online sales channel is switched on.
6Wine: does it have both tags?Fine Wines and its style tag. One alone is not enough.
7Does that page have four products?Under four and it redirects to its parent.
8Still nothing?Send us the product handle and what you expected. Do not start creating collections or changing handles.

9.1  A filter is empty but the products are there

Filters come from tags. An empty Country filter means those wines have no country tags. Add them — and remember rule 2: never remove the ones already there.

9.2  Something needs a new page or a new collection

Tell us. A new page is a website change, and it goes out with its collection in the same release so nobody lands on an empty page.
