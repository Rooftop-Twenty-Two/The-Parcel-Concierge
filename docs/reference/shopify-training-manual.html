Who this is for

Anyone at The Parcel Concierge who adds or edits products in Shopify. You do not need to understand the website to use it — follow the decision tree in section 4 and the product will land in the right place.

1  The one idea that explains everything

The website asks Shopify three separate questions about every product. Three questions, three different answers, three different things you control.

The questionWhat answers itWhat it controls
What page does it appear on? The collection it is in Whether it shows on /hampers, /wine, /occasions/christmas, a Build a Gift shelf, and so on. This is the big one.
What kind of thing is it? The type: tag Its web address, which page template it uses, and whether the over-18 gate appears.
How do shoppers narrow it down? All the other tags The filters down the side of a listing — grape, country, region, occasion, recipient.
The single most important consequence

A Shopify product CSV has no column for collections. So a spreadsheet import can classify and tag every product in the shop, and it still cannot put one single product onto a page. Collections are added in the Shopify admin, by hand or by an automated rule. Importing a CSV and expecting products to appear on the site will not work.

Said another way

Think of the website as a shop floor
The collection = which shelf you physically put the box on. If it is on no shelf, no customer walks past it.
The type: tag = the label on the box saying what it is, so the shop knows whether to card the buyer.
The other tags = the little signs a customer uses to find "red wine, French, under €25".

2  The seven rules

1
If it is not in a collection, it is not on the website. It can be published, in stock and perfectly tagged, and still be invisible. Search will find it; no page will list it.
2
Every product goes in at least one collection. Hampers and wines have their own. Everything else goes in all-gifts plus one Build a Gift shelf.
3
Never remove the old tags. Tags like "French Wines" and "Chenin Blanc Wine" are not legacy clutter — they are what fills the Country and Grape filters today. Add to them; do not tidy them away.
4
Never put type:builder-item on a real product. It removes the product from every listing, from search, from the sitemap and from Build a Gift. See section 6.
5
A page needs four products to open. Under four, the page redirects to its parent. This is deliberate — an empty page is worse than no page.
6
Out of stock means off the site. Listings and search hide anything unavailable. You do not need to unpublish it.
7
Changes take up to an hour to appear. The site caches pages for speed. Nothing is broken; wait, or ask Rooftop Twenty Two to clear it.

3  Adding a product

Two ways in: one at a time in the admin, or in bulk by CSV. The fields are the same.

3.1  The fields that matter to the website

FieldRequired?What the site does with it
TitleYesShown on the card and the product page. Also what site search matches on.
HandleAutoThe web address. Never change a handle on a live product — it breaks every existing link and any Google ranking.
DescriptionYesThe product page body. Worth writing properly; it is what ranks.
ImagesYesFirst image is the card image. Landscape 4:3 works best.
PriceYesPrice bands are worked out from this automatically. There is no price tag to set.
InventoryYesZero stock hides it everywhere.
PublishedYesUnpublished is invisible, full stop.
Product typeNoLeave blank unless it is a Build a Gift box, which uses GIST_BUILDER_PRODUCT. Do not set that yourself.
TagsYesSee section 6.
CollectionsYesSee sections 4 and 5. This is the one that decides where it shows.

3.2  Adding one product by hand

  1. Products → Add product. Fill in title, description, images, price, stock.
  2. Tags. Work through section 6 and add every tag that applies.
  3. Collections. In the right-hand panel, add it to the collections section 4 tells you. If a collection is automated, you cannot add it here — it joins by itself once the tags match.
  4. Save, then check it on the site within the hour.

3.3  Importing by CSV

Tick the overwrite box

On Products → Import, you must tick "Overwrite any current products that have the same handle".

Without it, Shopify silently skips every product whose handle already exists. On a re-tagging import that means almost nothing changes and the file looks like it did not work. This has already caught us once: a wine collection came back with 314 products instead of 373.

  1. Export first: Products → Export → All products → CSV. That is your rollback.
  2. Import the new file with the overwrite box ticked.
  3. Wait for the confirmation email. Large files take several minutes.
  4. Spot-check five products: right tags, right price, images still there.
  5. Now do the collections, which the CSV could not touch.

4  Where do I put it? The decision tree

Start at the top. The first line that matches is your answer.

Is it a finished hamper or gift box we sell as one product?
Yes → Collection: christmas-2026 (titled "General Hampers 2026")
Tag: type:hamper
Appears on: /hampers, the matching price band, the homepage hamper band, and /occasions/christmas. The price band is chosen automatically from the price — you do nothing.
Is it wine, Champagne, port or sherry?
Yes → Collections: fine-wines-1 and its colour or style collection (red-wines-1, white-wines-1, rose, champagne-and-prosecco-1, port-and-sherry-1, organic-wine, non-alcoholic-1, wine-gift-sets)
Tags: type:wine, contains:alcohol, plus country, region and grape
Appears on: /wine, the colour page, the right price band. Filters need the country/region/grape tags.
Is it a spirit or liqueur?
Yes → Collection: fine-wines-1
Tags: type:spirit, contains:alcohol
Anything else — chocolate, candles, crystal, baby, cards, homeware, food
→ Collections: all-gifts and one shelf from section 7
Tag: type:addon, plus contains:alcohol if it contains any
Appears in: Build a Gift, on the shelf you chose. It will not have a section of its own, and that is by design.
Why there is no gifts section any more

The site sells hampers and wine as its products. Everything else is sold as something you put into a hamper, through Build a Gift. A chocolate bar is not missing from the site because it has no gifts page — it is on the chocolate shelf of the builder, which is where it is meant to be.

The collection with the confusing name

The hamper collection's handle is christmas-2026 but its title is "General Hampers 2026". It was renamed in Shopify and Shopify keeps the original handle when you rename. The website asks for it by handle, so it still works.

Put every hamper in it, all year round — not only Christmas ones. It is the hamper range, despite the name. Renaming it will not change the handle; creating a new collection to replace it would empty the hamper pages, so please talk to us first.

6  The tag dictionary

Tags are case-sensitive in Shopify but the website is forgiving about spelling, case and accents on the wine tags. Type them exactly as shown anyway.

6.1  Kind tags — one per product

TagUse it forWhat it does
type:hamperFinished hampersAddress becomes /hampers/<name>; hamper template
type:wineWine, Champagne, port, sherryAddress becomes /wine/<name>; wine template; arms the over-18 gate
type:spiritSpirits and liqueursAs above, with the age gate
type:addonEverything elseMarks it as something that goes into a hamper
Never use type:builder-item

It exists for internal test data only. On the live shop it removes the product from the Build a Gift picker, from every listing, from search and from the sitemap, leaving it with no way to be bought at all. If you see it on a real product, take it off.

6.2  Filter tags

Tag familyAllowed valuesFills this filter
occasion: christmas, new-baby, sympathy, anniversary, thank-you, birthday, wedding, corporate "Occasion" on listings that show it
recipient: her, him, couple, family, client "Recipient"
Country words "France", "French Wines", "Italy", "Spain"… any spelling "Country" on wine listings
Region words "Bordeaux", "Rioja", "Chianti" — the region on its own "Region"
Grape words "Syrah", "Chenin Blanc Wine", "malbec"… any spelling "Grape"

Example: a French Bordeaux would carry France, Bordeaux and Cabernet Sauvignon — three plain tags, no prefix.

6.3  Behaviour tags

TagWhat it does
contains:alcohol Arms the over-18 gate for a Build a Gift item, and marks a corporate gift as alcoholic. Put it on beer, cider, liqueur chocolates and anything else with alcohol in it — these are missed most often.
badge:* Card flags. Leave these to us; badge:exclusive needs a matching setting the store does not have yet.

6.4  Tags the site ignores

TagWhy it does nothing
price:*Price bands are worked out from the actual price. Harmless but pointless.
aisle:*Nothing reads it. Do not add it.
Once more, because it is the expensive mistake

Tags do not put a product on a page. Only collections do. A wine tagged perfectly and in no collection appears on no wine page — that is exactly what happened to the Tokara Director's Reserve White, which was correctly tagged "White Wine" and "Fine Wines" and was in neither collection.

9  It is not showing up

Work down this list in order. It is ordered by how often each one is the cause.

#CheckFix
1Is it in a collection?Open the product, look at the Collections panel. Empty is the answer nine times out of ten. Add it per section 4.
2Has an hour passed?Pages are cached for up to an hour. Wait, or ask us to clear the cache.
3Is it in stock?Zero stock hides it from every listing and from search.
4Is it published?Products → the product → Publishing → make sure the online sales channel is on.
5Is it in the right collection?A wine in red-wines-1 but not fine-wines-1 is missing from /wine and every price band. Check both.
6Does the page have four products?Under four and the page redirects to its parent. Add more, or accept the page stays closed.
7Is the collection automated?If so, the product joins only when its tags match the rule. Check the rule, then the tags.
8Does it have type:builder-item?Remove it. See section 6.1.
9Still nothing?Send us the product handle and what you expected. Do not start changing handles.

9.1  The filter is empty but the products are there

Filters are fed by tags, not collections. An empty Country filter means the wines in that collection have no country tags. Add them — and remember rule 3: do not remove the old ones.

10.1  Switching one back on

  1. Products → Collections → Create collection.
  2. Title it as the table says, then check the handle at the bottom of the page under Search engine listing. It must match the first column exactly. Shopify adds -1 or -2 if the handle is taken, and a collection at the wrong handle is invisible to the site.
  3. Set it to Automated and add the condition in the last column.
  4. Check it holds at least four available products.
  5. Within the hour the page opens and rejoins the Occasions menu by itself. Nothing needs deploying.
The handle is the thing that matters

The title is for shoppers; the handle is what the website asks for. This is exactly how the hamper collection ended up named "General Hampers 2026" while the site asks for christmas-2026 — the title changed, the handle did not.

A manual collection cannot be converted to automated after the fact. If you make it manual and want a rule later, you have to delete and recreate — and then watch the handle, because the old one will be taken.

10.2  Still to create, never existed

sympathy → /occasions/sympathy  ·  get-well → /occasions/get-well  ·  exclusive-to-us → /wine/exclusive-to-us

Same procedure. All three pages are built and waiting.

In short

Collection = which page. type: tag = what it is. Other tags = the filters. If you remember only that, and rule 1, you will not go far wrong.
